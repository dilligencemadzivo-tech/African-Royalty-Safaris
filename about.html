Our Story & Philosophy

Welcome to African Royalty Safaris

Founded with a deep love for Tanzania's breathtaking landscapes and rich wildlife, African Royalty Safaris has been creating exceptional safari experiences for over 15 years. What began as a passion project between local guides has grown into one of Tanzania's most respected luxury safari companies.

Our name reflects our commitment to providing royal treatment to every guest who travels with us. We believe that exploring Africa's wonders should be an experience of comfort, wonder, and deep connection – not just a tour.

"Our mission is to create transformative journeys that connect travelers with the heart of Africa while preserving its natural beauty for generations to come."

Based in Arusha, the safari capital of Tanzania, we have intimate knowledge of every national park, wildlife migration pattern, and cultural experience that makes Tanzania truly special. Our team combines decades of guiding experience with modern hospitality standards to deliver safaris that exceed expectations.

Our Commitment to Sustainability

We believe that tourism should preserve and protect the very wonders that travelers come to experience. Our sustainability initiatives focus on three key areas:

  • Environmental Conservation: We follow strict eco-friendly practices, minimize single-use plastics, and contribute to wildlife conservation projects.
  • Community Empowerment: We employ local guides, support community-run lodges, and invest in education and healthcare projects in rural areas.
  • Cultural Preservation: We promote authentic cultural exchanges that respect traditions while providing economic benefits to local communities.
