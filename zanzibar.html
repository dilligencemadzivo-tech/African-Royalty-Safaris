About Zanzibar

Zanzibar is an archipelago off the coast of Tanzania, famous for its beautiful white-sand beaches, turquoise waters, and rich cultural heritage. Often called the "Spice Islands," Zanzibar has a fascinating history shaped by Arab, Persian, Indian, and European influences.

With African Royalty Safaris, you'll discover the best of Zanzibar - from the historic Stone Town with its labyrinthine alleys to the idyllic beaches of Nungwi and Kendwa. Our carefully curated experiences blend relaxation, adventure, and cultural immersion for an unforgettable island getaway.

Zanzibar's Best Beaches

Discover paradise on these stunning shores

Nungwi & Kendwa

Located at the northern tip of Zanzibar, these beaches feature powdery white sand and turquoise waters. Ideal for swimming at any tide and famous for spectacular sunsets.

Paje & Jambiani

On the southeast coast, these long stretches of beach are known for their turquoise waters and traditional fishing villages. Perfect for kite surfing and cultural experiences.

Matemwe

A peaceful fishing village with a beautiful beach overlooking Mnemba Atoll. Excellent for snorkeling, diving, and experiencing authentic village life.

Michamvi & Bwejuu

These secluded beaches on the east coast offer privacy and tranquility. Known for their stunning sunrise views and excellent swimming conditions.

Kiwengwa & Uroa

Mid-east coast beaches with calm waters and a range of accommodation options. Great for families and water sports enthusiasts.

Mnemba Island

An exclusive private island surrounded by a marine conservation area. Offers some of Zanzibar's best snorkeling and diving with abundant marine life.
