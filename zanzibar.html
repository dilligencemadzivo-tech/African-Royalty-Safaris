The Spice Island Paradise

Zanzibar is an archipelago off the coast of Tanzania, famous for its breathtaking white-sand beaches, turquoise waters, and rich cultural heritage. Often called the "Spice Islands," Zanzibar has a fascinating history shaped by Arab, Persian, Indian, and European influences.

With African Royalty Safaris, you'll discover the authentic Zanzibar - from the historic Stone Town with its labyrinthine alleys to the idyllic beaches of Nungwi and Kendwa. Our carefully curated experiences blend relaxation, adventure, and cultural immersion for an unforgettable island getaway.

"Zanzibar offers the perfect combination of tropical luxury, cultural richness, and natural beauty - creating memories that last a lifetime."

Whether you're seeking romantic seclusion, family adventure, or cultural exploration, our expert guides will create a personalized itinerary that captures the true essence of this magical island.

Best Time to Visit

Plan your perfect Zanzibar getaway based on the season

Dry Season

June - October

Perfect weather with clear skies, low humidity, and excellent conditions for beach activities and water sports.

Short Rains

November - December

Brief showers with plenty of sunshine. Lower prices and fewer crowds make this a great value season.

Long Rains

March - May

Heavier rainfall but still plenty of sunny intervals. Ideal for indoor cultural experiences and spa retreats.

Hot Season

January - February

Warmest months with calm seas. Perfect for diving, snorkeling, and enjoying the vibrant island atmosphere.
