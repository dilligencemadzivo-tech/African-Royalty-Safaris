Your Preparation Guide

Follow our comprehensive guide to prepare for your Kilimanjaro adventure

Physical Training

Begin training 3-4 months in advance with cardio exercises, strength training, and hiking with a weighted pack. Aim for 4-5 workouts per week including long hikes on weekends.

Gear & Equipment

We provide a detailed gear checklist. Essential items include broken-in hiking boots, layered clothing system, quality sleeping bag rated to -10°C, headlamp, and trekking poles.

Health & Medication

Consult your doctor about altitude medication (Diamox is recommended). Get necessary vaccinations, travel insurance with mountain rescue, and bring personal medications.

Travel Documents

Ensure passport validity (6+ months), obtain Tanzania tourist visa, carry travel insurance documents, vaccination certificates, and emergency contact information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about climbing Kilimanjaro

The best climbing seasons are January-March and June-October. These dry seasons offer the best weather conditions with clearer skies and less precipitation. December can also be good, while April-May and November see more rainfall.

Kilimanjaro is considered a strenuous trek rather than a technical climb. The main challenge is altitude. With proper preparation, pacing, and acclimatization, most determined individuals in good physical condition can reach the summit. Our success rate is 98% with proper route selection.

Our all-inclusive packages cover: All park fees and permits, professional mountain guides and porters, quality camping equipment, all meals on the mountain, airport transfers, pre-climb briefing, and emergency oxygen. Premium packages include additional comforts like private toilet tents, better accommodation, and extra services.

We recommend 3-4 months of preparation focusing on cardiovascular fitness (running, cycling, stair climbing), leg strength (squats, lunges), and endurance hiking with a weighted pack. Aim to comfortably hike 15-20km with elevation gain while carrying 8-10kg. We provide a detailed training plan upon booking.
